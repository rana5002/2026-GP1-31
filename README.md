Ufuq 
-----------------------

Introduction
-----------------------

Ufuq is a graduation project a web-based platform centralizing training programs and professional courses for students, graduates, and professionals in computing-related fields in Saudi Arabia. Verified companies publish their own training programs and courses directly on the platform, and admins can also add opportunities. Users build a profile of their skills and experience, and the platform helps them browse, evaluate, and apply to opportunities that fit them.

The platform supports three roles: students who browse and apply to opportunities, companies who register and publish their own training programs and courses after admin verification, and admins who verify companies, add opportunities, and oversee what gets published on the platform.
