About Ufuq

One organized door into training and courses.

Ufuq (أُفق — "horizon") brings Saudi Arabia's training programs and professional courses for computing-field students into a single, trustworthy platform — so finding the right next step doesn't mean checking a dozen different company pages and group chats.

Our Mission

Make it effortless for Saudi computing students to discover, compare and apply to real training opportunities and courses.

Verified by Design

Every company on Ufuq is reviewed by an admin before it can publish — so what you see is trustworthy.

Built for Computing Fields

Focused specifically on IT, Computer Science, Computer Engineering and related fields of study.

Why "Ufuq"?

Ufuq is the Arabic word for horizon — the line where opportunity becomes visible. The logo's open door reflects that idea: a single, clear way through to what's next in your career.

A graduation project, built to actually be used

Ufuq is developed as an IT496 graduation project, with real account roles, company verification, and a growing set of AI-assisted features — recommendations, skill-gap analysis, and opportunity summaries.
